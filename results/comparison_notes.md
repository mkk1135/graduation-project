## What the comparison shows

Notes on the tables above. Every number in them is filled in from the results database. Statistical tests are exact McNemar tests on the same images; no correction for multiple comparisons was applied, and only the two primary comparisons were fixed in advance in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).

1. **Ranking on the final test set (384 images).** Claude Sonnet 5.5 96.6%, the combination 95.3%, the Community Forensics detector 92.2%, the published CNN 81.5%, the four other published detectors 50.0% to 75.8%. Primary comparisons: the combination is more accurate than the Community Forensics detector as distributed (p = 0.004); the published CNN (setting 5, tuned threshold) cannot be told apart from the CNN of the first round, setting 4 (82.3%, p = 0.791).
2. **Secondary comparison: the published detector with a tuned threshold.** With its threshold chosen on the development images by the same rule as the project's own detectors, the Community Forensics detector reaches 94.0%. The combination is not significantly better than that (p = 0.302). Much of the advantage of the combination over the detector as distributed therefore comes from the threshold, not from the CNN.
3. **JPEG re-compression at quality 90.** The Community Forensics detector falls from 92.2% to 82.3% (tuned threshold: 88.5%), the combination from 95.3% to 90.1%, the published CNN from 81.5% to 78.1%. After re-compression the combination is clearly better than the detector as distributed (p < 0.001), but not than the tuned one (p = 0.238).
4. **ChatGPT and Gemini set (168 images).** Claude Sonnet 5.5 is the most accurate (85.1%), followed by the Ateeqq detector (82.1%). The combination scores 69.0%: it accepts 71 photographs and misses 1, but recognises only 45 generated images and misses 51. The Community Forensics detector scores 57.7% and recognises 26 generated images.
5. **Reddit set (120 images).** Claude Sonnet 5.5 is again the most accurate (96.7%), followed by the Ateeqq detector (83.3%) and the CNN of the first round (79.2%). The combination scores 70.8%: it accepts 59 photographs and misses 1, but recognises only 26 generated images and misses 34. No detector was the best on all three held-out sets.
6. **Training setting and threshold, separated.** Accuracy of the four variants on the final test set, the ChatGPT and Gemini set and the Reddit set: setting 4 with the default threshold 82.3%, 73.2% and 79.2%; setting 4 with a tuned threshold 83.1%, 69.6% and 71.7%; setting 5 with the default threshold 82.8%, 64.9% and 68.3%; setting 5 with a tuned threshold (the published CNN) 81.5%, 68.5% and 68.3%. On the final test set the four are close (setting 5 against setting 4, both at the default threshold: p = 0.888). On the two other sets setting 4 with the default threshold is the most accurate of the four, and setting 5 at the default threshold is less accurate than it (p = 0.020 and p = 0.002). On these two sets the change of training setting lowered accuracy; the tuned threshold lowered that of setting 4 as well, and changed that of setting 5 little.
7. **An answer "generated" was more reliable than an answer "real".** When the combination called an image generated, it was right for 98% of those images on the final test set, 98% on the ChatGPT and Gemini set and 96% on the Reddit set. When it called an image real, it was right for 93%, 58% and 63%. On new kinds of image, "real" means only that no known trace was found.
8. **Limits.** The sets are small: the 95% confidence interval of the combination is 92.7 to 97.0% on the final test set, 61.7 to 75.5% on the ChatGPT and Gemini set and 62.2 to 78.2% on the Reddit set. In the Reddit set the class of an image is the kind of subreddit it was posted in, so some labels may be wrong. Claude Sonnet 5.5 was asked once per image and saw JPEG copies; whether any test image was in its training data, or in that of the published detectors, is not known. The two repeated training runs (seeds 1 and 2) are not yet included, so the variation between runs is not known.
