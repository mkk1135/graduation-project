AI-Generated Image Detection System

Choose an image to check whether it is a real photograph or AI-generated. The image is analysed inside your browser, on your own device, and is not sent anywhere.

The answer is an indication, not proof. On a test of 384 images the detector was wrong about one time in five, and it tends to call small, blurred or heavily edited photographs AI-generated.

The detector is mkk1135/ai-image-detector, a ConvNeXt-Tiny network fine-tuned for a Bachelor of Cybersecurity graduation project. The code, the lists of test images and all results are at github.com/mkk1135/graduation-project. The first visit downloads the detector (about 110 MB). An image takes from a few seconds to about a minute, depending on its size and on your device.
